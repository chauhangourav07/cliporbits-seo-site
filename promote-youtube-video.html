Frequently asked

What's the real difference between the two?
Starter promotes one video. Growth coordinates targeting across three at once, more efficient if you already have a batch ready.
Is ad spend really included?
Yes, in both one-time packages. (Retainers bill ad spend separately.)
How many views will I get?
It depends mostly on which countries you target and how well your video works as an ad, so we don't promise a fixed number. Before launch we set a realistic expectation for your video, and your report shows the exact views Google Ads delivered. If delivery falls short of what your budget should reasonably produce, the Verified Delivery Guarantee applies. Want an estimate first? Send us your video and target country on WhatsApp.
Is this safe for my channel?
Yes. Your video runs as an ad through Google Ads, YouTube's own advertising system and the same one brands use every day, and Google only counts a view when a person chooses to watch. What puts channels at risk is bots, view exchanges and fake engagement, and we never use any of them.
Which countries can you target?
Almost any country Google Ads serves: one country, several, or a whole region. We've run campaigns for clients in 40+ countries. Views cost more in some countries, like the US or UK, than in others, so the same budget goes further in some markets, and we'll tell you what to expect for yours before launch.
What if I'm not happy with the results?
If you change your mind before your campaign starts, you can cancel for a refund. Once it's running, the Verified Delivery Guarantee applies: if delivery falls short of what your budget should reasonably produce, we keep the ads running at no extra cost or refund the shortfall, whichever you prefer.
Is there a subscription?
No, both are one-time purchases. For ongoing management, see Growth Plans.
Looking to buy YouTube subscribers instead of views?
A direct subscriber purchase is always a bot or an empty promise, and no legitimate service can guarantee a real subscribe action. Real ad-driven viewers have the opportunity to become subscribers if they genuinely value the content, but we never guarantee a specific subscriber count. Read the full answer →
