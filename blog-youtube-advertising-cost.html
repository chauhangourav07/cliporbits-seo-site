How Much Does YouTube Advertising Cost in 2026?

By Gourav Chauhan · Updated November 17, 2026

YouTube advertising has no set price. You choose the budget, Google runs an auction for every ad slot, and you pay either per view or per 1,000 impressions, depending on the ad format. In our own 2026 campaigns, a view costs about $0.01 to $0.03 when the viewers are in the US or UK and under $0.002 in lower-cost countries. Your total is the budget you set, plus a fee only if someone else runs the campaign for you.

Key takeaways
  • YouTube ads are bought through Google Ads in an auction, and Google publishes no rate card.
  • Skippable and in-feed ads are usually paid per view. Bumper and non-skippable ads are paid per 1,000 impressions.
  • In our 2026 campaigns, US and UK views cost $0.01 to $0.03 each, and views in lower-cost countries under $0.002.
  • On top of the ad budget, you pay a management fee only if you hire someone to run the campaign.

How YouTube charges you: per view or per 1,000 impressions

The ad format decides how you're billed. With cost-per-view (CPV) bidding, you pay for views and interactions, such as clicks on the ad. Bumper and non-skippable ads use target CPM, so you pay based on impressions: the number of times the ad is shown. What counts as a view also changes by format, according to Google's view rules.

What that means for your money: on a skippable ad with per-view billing, a viewer who skips before 30 seconds without clicking costs you nothing. On a CPM ad, you pay for the impression whether or not the viewer cares. If you want people to actually watch your video, per-view billing usually fits better. CPM suits short messages meant to reach as many people as possible.

How to set a budget that won't surprise you

In Google Ads you set an average daily budget. Spend moves around from day to day, but Google says you will never pay more than two times your average daily budget on any one day, or 30.4 times it in a month, for most campaigns. A $10 daily budget, for example, can't cost more than $304 in a month. YouTube Promote uses a total budget instead, and Google's playbook says you'll never be charged more than the total you set.

To estimate what a budget buys, divide it by the cost per view. At $0.01 to $0.03 per US or UK view, $300 buys roughly 10,000 to 30,000 views. Our guide to the cost to promote a YouTube video works through more budgets and countries.

Then start small. Run one video for a few days, check in YouTube Studio how long ad viewers stay, and only then put more behind it. A cheap view is only a good view if the person watches.

What ad spend can't buy

Ads bring real viewers, but two things stay off the table. Watch hours and subscribers from ad campaigns don't count toward the YouTube Partner Program, according to YouTube's Promote help page. We explain the rule in do views from YouTube ads count toward monetization? And nobody can guarantee you subscribers, because subscribing is always the viewer's choice.

How to avoid wasting your budget

Whichever option you choose, the biggest budget-waster is not being able to check whether the money went where it was supposed to. Ask any provider one direct question: "Will you show me the Google Ads delivery numbers, and can I check them against my own YouTube Studio?" A provider who can't or won't answer that is making verification harder than it needs to be.

FAQ

How much does a YouTube ad cost per view?
In our 2026 campaigns, $0.01 to $0.03 per view when the viewers are in the US or UK, and under $0.002 in lower-cost countries. Google doesn't publish prices, so the auction and your targeting decide the final number.
What's the difference between CPV and CPM on YouTube?
With CPV (cost per view) you pay when someone watches: on a skippable ad, that means 30 seconds, the whole ad if it's shorter, or a click. With CPM you pay per 1,000 impressions, whether or not anyone watches. Bumper and non-skippable ads use CPM.
Is there a minimum budget for YouTube ads?
You choose the budget yourself. For YouTube Promote, Google's playbook says you can get results from $5 and recommends at least $100. In Google Ads, a single day can't cost more than two times your average daily budget.
How much do YouTube marketing agencies charge?
Most full-service agencies quote privately: a monthly retainer on top of your ad spend. ClipOrbits Growth Plans are $349, $699 or $1,299 a month with ad spend billed separately, and a single video starts at $49 with ad spend included.
Is YouTube advertising worth the cost?
For a video that already holds viewers but isn't being found, usually yes: ads put it in front of people who choose to watch. If viewers leave early anyway, fix the video first. And ad views don't count toward monetization.
What's the cheapest way to advertise on YouTube?
Running a small campaign yourself in YouTube Promote or Google Ads costs only the ad budget. If you'd rather not learn the platform, our Starter Push is $49 for one video with the ad spend included.
