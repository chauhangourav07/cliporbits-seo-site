How Much Does It Cost to Get 1,000 YouTube Subscribers With Ads?

By Gourav Chauhan · Published October 12, 2026

It depends mostly on where your viewers are. In one of our worldwide campaigns, 7,381 people subscribed for $891 of ad spend, about $0.12 each, so 1,000 subscribers cost roughly $120. Aim only at the US or UK and the same math lands in the thousands. One catch before you budget: YouTube doesn't count subscribers from ads toward the Partner Program's 1,000.

Key takeaways
  • In one worldwide campaign of ours, a new subscriber cost about $0.12, roughly $120 per 1,000.
  • Targeting only the US or UK raises the cost per subscriber sharply, because each view costs far more.
  • Subscribers from ads are real people, but YouTube doesn't count them toward Partner Program eligibility.
  • No legitimate service can guarantee a subscriber count; subscribing is always the viewer's decision.

The formula

Cost per subscriber comes down to two numbers: what each view costs, and how many ad viewers it takes to get one subscriber.

Cost per subscriber = cost per view × views per new subscriber

In our worldwide campaign, Google Ads delivered 3,599,140 views for $891, and YouTube Studio recorded 7,381 new subscribers. That's about one subscriber for every 490 views, at about $0.00025 per view: roughly $0.12 per subscriber. You can see both sides of those numbers in the campaign report.

What raises your subscribe rate

  • Promote a video that represents the channel. YouTube's own advice for subscriber campaigns is to pick a video that shows viewers what kind of content they'll get.
  • Win the first 30 seconds. On skippable ads, a viewer who doesn't stay that long usually doesn't subscribe either.
  • Give them a reason to stay. A channel page with several videos on the same topic converts better than one video on its own.
  • Target people who already like the topic. Relevant interests beat the broadest possible audience for subscribers.

The monetization catch

Subscribers from ads are real people, and they show up in your subscriber count. But YouTube's Promote help page says only organic subscribers count when it checks Partner Program eligibility. If your goal is the 1,000 subscribers for monetization, ads won't get you there directly. We explain the full rule in do views from YouTube ads count toward monetization?

Why guaranteed subscriber packages are a red flag

Nobody can make a real person subscribe. A package that promises an exact number of subscribers is describing bot accounts, which YouTube's fake engagement policy prohibits. See can you buy YouTube subscribers? and 7 red flags of a bot-selling service.

FAQ

Can I buy 1,000 subscribers to get monetized?
No. YouTube counts only organic subscribers for Partner Program eligibility, and bot subscribers break YouTube's fake engagement policy. Ads bring real subscribers, but they don't count toward the threshold.
How do I see how many subscribers came from my ads?
Google Ads and YouTube Promote both report subscribers from your ads. Promote counts people who subscribe within three days of watching 10 seconds of the promoted video or clicking it.
Are subscribers from ads real?
Yes. YouTube says views and subscribers from Promote campaigns are legitimate: real viewers who chose to watch and subscribe.
Is cost per subscriber the best way to judge a campaign?
It's one number. Watch time, and whether new subscribers keep watching your next videos, matter more, because that's what helps the channel grow organically afterward.
