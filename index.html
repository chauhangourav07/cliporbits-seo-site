The Core Problem

Good Content Isn't the Problem. Knowing If Promotion Actually Works Is.

You already have videos worth watching, and the harder part is getting them in front of people who don't know your channel exists yet, and knowing whether the money you spend on promotion is doing anything. Bot networks report numbers that don't mean anything. Self-serve platforms leave you guessing at targeting and results. Enterprise agencies charge $20,000+/month, built for venture-backed brands, not a single channel. None of them show you proof from two independent sources before you have to trust the result.

That's why ClipOrbits checks every campaign against your Google Ads dashboard and YouTube Analytics, side by side.
