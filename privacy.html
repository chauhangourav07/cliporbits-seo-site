Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

ClipOrbits ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you interact with our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact form data: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, YouTube channel or video URL, budget, goals and any message you submit.
  • Communication data: emails, WhatsApp messages and call notes related to your inquiry or campaign.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and general usage information.
  • Campaign data: information you provide about your YouTube channel or content in order to plan, launch and optimize campaigns.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare personalized growth strategies.
  • To plan, launch, monitor and optimize Google Ads campaigns you request.
  • To send transactional information such as reports, invoices and campaign updates.
  • To improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

3. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to keep the site functional, and optional analytics or advertising cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

4. Google Analytics

If enabled, Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, time on site and general location. This data helps us improve the site. Google's own privacy policy applies to data processed by Google.

5. Meta Pixel

If enabled, the Meta Pixel collects information about your interaction with our site so we can measure ad performance and reach relevant audiences on Meta platforms. Meta's privacy policy applies to data processed by Meta.

6. How we protect your information

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS) and limited internal access. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information at all times.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (for example, Google Ads or payment processors) strictly to deliver the services you request, or where required by law.

8. Your rights

  • Access, correct or delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority where applicable.

9. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at contact@cliporbits.com.
