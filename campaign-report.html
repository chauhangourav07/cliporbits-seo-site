What each side of the ledger shows

Google Ads Dashboard shows what the campaign purchased: how many people were served the ad (impressions), how many chose to watch (views), what share of impressions converted to a view (view rate), and total spend.

YouTube Studio Analytics shows what happened on the channel afterward: total views on the promoted video, cumulative watch time, new subscribers gained, and how much of the video people actually watched on average.

If a campaign were faked or inflated, these two independent sources wouldn't line up. Here, they do, which is the entire point of checking both.

One result, not a guarantee

This is a single, real campaign. Results vary by channel, niche, video quality and budget, so we don't present this as a typical or guaranteed outcome, only as proof of what the reporting process looks like when you run one with us.
